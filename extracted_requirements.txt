FREIGHT FORWARDINGMANAGEMENT SYSTEM
Software Requirements & Development Blueprint
A practical, job-centric system for freight forwarding operations,shipment management, costing, revenue and profitability.
Prepared: 15 October 2026
1. Executive Summary
The proposed Freight Forwarding Management System (FFMS) is a small, professional and user-friendly application designed for daily freight forwarding operations. It will manage customers, jobs/shipments, shipment details, containers, buying costs, selling revenue, documents, reporting and automatic job profitability.
The recommended architecture is job-centric: every shipment/job acts as the central record to which shipment information, containers, buying charges, selling charges, documents, milestones and profitability are attached.

3. Automatic Profit Calculation
Core formula:
PROFIT = TOTAL SELLING AMOUNT − TOTAL BUYING AMOUNT
Whenever a buying or selling charge changes, the system must recalculate total buying, total selling and profit automatically. Direct manual editing of calculated profit should not be permitted for ordinary users.

21. Recommended Implementation Strategy
Build Version 1 around the Job File rather than as a simple shipment register. Every job should connect Customer → Shipment → Containers → Buying → Selling → Documents → Milestones → Profit. Once this foundation is stable, add quotations, rate management, tracking, accounting integration and customer-facing features.
22. Proposed Future Architecture
The system should be designed so that future integrations can include Tally Prime, email, WhatsApp, customer portal, shipment tracking APIs and relevant customs/FBR integrations.
23. Final Recommendation
The recommended solution is a web-based, multi-user Freight Forwarding Management System using React/Next.js, Django/REST and PostgreSQL. The first release should focus on operational simplicity, accurate job costing, automatic profitability, searchable records, reporting, security and backups. The application should be tested against defined acceptance cases before final delivery.
